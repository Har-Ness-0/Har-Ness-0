## 👋 About

I'm a full-stack engineer who builds AI into real products. My work sits where the model meets the user: running models in **Python**, serving them through **FastAPI** and **Node.js**, and shipping them in **Next.js** web apps and native **iOS** apps.

- 🔭 Currently building **Forensics AR**, which turns an iPhone room scan into a correctly measured 3D replica
- 🧠 Focused on computer vision, 3D reconstruction and AI-integrated SaaS
- 🎓 Studying at **Itahari International College**, in partnership with **London Metropolitan University**
- 🤝 Open to internships, freelance work and collaboration

### 🧊 Forensics AR
Scan a room on an iPhone and rebuild it as a **measured 3D model**. On-device depth estimation (Depth Anything V2 via Core ML) feeds a FastAPI service that fuses frames into a mesh with Open3D, rendered in Three.js.

`Expo` `Swift` `ARKit` `Core ML` `FastAPI` `Open3D` `Three.js`

### 🍽️ Food → Nutrition AI Pipeline
Estimates the nutritional value of a meal from a photo or video. **SAM2** segments each food item, **Depth Anything V2** estimates depth, a scale module recovers real-world size, and volume is converted to grams and matched against USDA FoodData Central.

`Python` `PyTorch` `Hugging Face` `SAM2` `Computer Vision`

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-in%20development-D29922?style=flat-square)

</td>

### 🪰 FlyBot
An ESP32 robot driven by a **fruit-fly-inspired neural controller** running in the cloud. Sensor data streams over MQTT to an Azure-hosted "brain" that sends motor commands back in real time.

`ESP32` `C++` `MQTT` `Azure` `Python`
